## Project Overview

Student Success Hub is a hypothetical educational website designed to help provide students with useful academic resources and information in one main central location.

The website aims to make academic information easier to access by students by providing study resources, academic information, an academic calendar, frequently asked questions, and ways for students to contact the organisation.

The target audience is mainly students who need easy access to educational resources and support information.

---

## Website Goals and Objectives

The main goals of Student Success Hub are to:

- Provide students with easily accessible academic resources.
- Organise useful study information in one central location.
- Provide information about academic activities and important dates.
- Allow students to make enquiries and contact the organisation.
- Create a simple and user-friendly navigation system.
- Provide a professional foundation that can be developed further in later parts of the POE assesment.

---

## Target Audience

The primary target audience is students who require academic resources, study information and support.

The website is designed to be simple to navigate so that users can quickly locate the information they need.

## Key Features and Functionality
